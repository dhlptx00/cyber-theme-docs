Cyber documentation

Cyber is a high-contrast HUD-style Shopify Online Store 2.0 theme for electronics, tech accessories and other spec-heavy catalogs. It was written from scratch, with no Dawn or other base theme. The focus is speed: fast filtering, readable specs, and bulk ordering.

Version1.0.0
Authordhlptx00
PlatformShopify Online Store 2.0
UpdatedOctober 08, 2026

This manual is written for store owners and assumes no coding knowledge. Every section, block and setting in the theme editor is listed in Sections and blocks.

Requirements

  • A Shopify store on any plan that includes the Online Store sales channel.
  • Optional: the free Shopify Search & Discovery app, which powers the filter console.
  • Supported browsers: the latest versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge, plus mobile Safari, Chrome Mobile and Samsung Internet.

Installation

  1. Open Cyber in the Shopify Theme Store and click Try theme to add it to your theme library for free. You can preview and customize it before you buy.
  2. In Shopify admin go to Online Store › Themes. Cyber appears in the Theme library.
  3. Click Customize to open the theme editor, or ⋯ › Preview to look around.
  4. When you're ready, click Publish. Shopify asks you to buy the theme at that point. One purchase covers one store.
Updates. When a new version of Cyber is released, Shopify shows Update available on the theme in your theme library. Updating creates a copy of the theme with your settings and content carried over, so your live store isn't touched until you publish the update. Code edits you made yourself aren't carried over, so note them before updating.

Quick setup (about 15 minutes)

  1. Logo and favicon: Theme settings › Logo and favicon. Without a logo, Cyber shows a monogram plus your store name.
  2. Main menu: build a nested menu (see Navigation).
  3. Filters: install Search & Discovery and add filters (see Filters).
  4. Quick order page: create a page and assign the page.quick-order template (see Quick order).
  5. Contact page: create a page called Contact and assign the page.contact template.
  6. Specs: add the three product metafield definitions (see Metafields), or import the demo CSV.
  7. Home page: in the theme editor pick a collection for Product bento and edit the hero copy.

Style and colors

Cyber ships with one style, Cyber, a dark style with seven color schemes. Schemes 6 and 7 are light (off-white base and white panel). For a light store, open the theme editor, go to Theme settings › Colors, and set Body color scheme to scheme 6. You can also assign the light schemes to individual sections. Each section's Color scheme setting picks any of the seven.

Each style has five color schemes. Every section has a Color scheme setting:

SchemeUse
Scheme 1 · BasePage background and most sections
Scheme 2 · Yellow slabHero slab, highlights, CTA bands
Scheme 3 · PanelRaised panels and alternating sections
Scheme 4 · Red alertSale and warning bands
Scheme 5 · Cyan dataData callouts and stats

Each scheme has its own background, surface, text, muted, line, accent, on-accent, highlight, data, alert and on-alert colors. The defaults meet WCAG AA contrast. If you change them, keep body text at a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1.

Effects (Theme settings › Effects): scanlines, glitch hover, grid background, HUD corners and corner-cut size. All motion is turned off for visitors who ask for reduced motion.

Navigation and mega menu

  1. Go to Online Store › Navigation and open Main menu.
  2. Add top-level items, for example Home, Catalog, Blog and Contact.
  3. Drag items under a parent to nest them, for example Cables, Chargers, Power Banks, Cases and Adapters under Catalog. Any top-level item with children opens a mega panel. Third-level links show indented.
  4. Optional: in the theme editor open Header, add a Mega menu block and type the exact top-level title (for example Catalog) in Menu item. Add an image, heading, text and collection button for the feature column.
  5. Optional: add Link blocks to the header for extra items such as Quick order.

On desktop the panel opens on hover or click. On touch screens it opens on tap inside the menu drawer. Esc closes it, and every link can be reached with the keyboard.

Product page

  • Variant pills and swatches. Sold-out and unavailable combinations are crossed out. Add to cart and the dynamic checkout buttons turn off when a variant can't be bought.
  • Stock readout. Shows OK, LOW n or OUT. LOW appears at or below Theme settings › Cart › Low stock threshold. Only real inventory is used, never fake scarcity.
  • Product card badges. Tag a product flagship or pro to get a rarity strip, and new to get a NEW badge. Sale badges come from compare-at prices.
  • Spec readout, Compatibility and Paired products are separate sections below the product information, so you can reorder or remove them.

Product metafields

Three optional metafields drive the spec features. Create them under Settings › Custom data › Products › Add definition, using exactly these namespaces and keys:

NameNamespace and keyTypeUsed by
Specs tablespecs.tableJSONSpec readout table, spec line on cards, quick-order spec column
Compatibilityspecs.compatibilitySingle line text, list of valuesCompatibility chips and check-my-device
Statsspecs.statsJSONStat bars on cards and the radial gauge

specs.table example

[
  {"label": "Connector",  "value": "USB-C to USB-C"},
  {"label": "Max power",  "value": "100W (20V/5A, E-marker)"},
  {"label": "Data speed", "value": "USB 2.0, 480 Mbps"},
  {"label": "Jacket",     "value": "Braided nylon"}
]

specs.stats example

pct is the bar fill from 0 to 100. Keep it to three or four stats.

[
  {"label": "POWER",      "value": "100W",    "pct": 42},
  {"label": "SPEED",      "value": "480Mbps", "pct": 15},
  {"label": "DURABILITY", "value": "20K",     "pct": 72}
]

specs.compatibility example

One device per value:

MacBook Air / Pro (USB-C)
iPhone 15 / 16 series
Samsung Galaxy S23–S25
If a metafield is empty, the section uses its editor blocks instead (Row or Device), or hides itself, so nothing breaks.

Search and Discovery filters

  1. Install the free Search & Discovery app from the Shopify App Store.
  2. Open Search & Discovery › Filters › Add filter. Recommended filters: Availability, Price, Product type, Vendor, Tag, and option filters such as Color, Length and Model.
  3. Save. The filter console on collection and search pages picks them up automatically, with chips, swatches for Color options, a price range and an in-stock toggle.

Filtering and sorting update the results without reloading the page, and the URL updates so filtered views can be shared. The grid/list toggle is remembered per visitor. List view adds a variant select, quantity and add to cart on every row, which suits repeat B2B buyers.

Quick order page

  1. Go to Online Store › Pages › Add page and name it Quick Order.
  2. Under Theme template choose quick-order and save.
  3. In the theme editor open the page and pick the Collection to list. The default is all.
  4. Add it to the main menu or as a header Link block.

Shoppers can filter by SKU or title, use category chips, set quantities (capped at available stock), or paste lines such as CL-CC100-1M-BLACK x3, VP-GAN65-EU-WHITE,2 or SKU 5. Add all to cart sends everything in one request. The page lists up to 50 products (all their variants) per collection, so for larger catalogs make one quick-order page per collection.

Blog and articles

Turn on comments under Online Store › Blog posts › Manage blogs. The comment form shows when comments are on, and shows the right confirmation for moderated and unmoderated blogs. Read time is estimated at 200 words per minute.

Performance and accessibility

Cyber uses vanilla JavaScript with no frameworks, per-area CSS, lazy responsive images and fonts from the Shopify font library. Lighthouse results on the demo catalog: desktop performance 99–100 and mobile performance 84–85 (home, product, collection), with accessibility at 96–97. Most of the remaining mobile cost comes from Shopify's own scripts (analytics and the Shopify account component), which every theme loads.

  • Keyboard navigation everywhere, including mega panels and the filter console. Focus is always visible.
  • All animations respect reduced-motion preferences.
  • Upload product images at 1200px or larger. Cyber serves the right size for each screen.

FAQ

The mega menu doesn't open.

The top-level item needs child links in Online Store › Navigation, or a matching Mega menu block in the header. The block's Menu item must match the title exactly (case doesn't matter).

The filter console is empty.

Add filters in the Search & Discovery app. Shopify only shows filters for values that exist in the collection.

Specs or compatibility don't show.

Check that the metafield definitions use the namespace specs and the keys table, stats and compatibility, with the types listed above. JSON must be valid. Paste it into a JSON validator if unsure.

How do I change fonts?

Theme settings › Typography has three font pickers (display, body and mono) from the Shopify font library.

Can I turn off the scanlines or glitch effect?

Yes. Theme settings › Effects has a toggle for each effect.

Quick order shows only some products.

It lists up to 50 products per page. Make one quick-order page per collection for big catalogs.

Does Cyber need any apps?

No. Search & Discovery (free, made by Shopify) is recommended for filters, but nothing requires a paid app.

Can I edit the code?

Yes, but always duplicate the theme first (Themes › ⋯ › Duplicate). Custom code changes aren't covered by support. Consider hiring a Shopify Partner for larger changes.
